# Charged Particles

The normalized momentum is $\mathbf{u} = \gamma\boldsymbol{\beta}$, with
$\gamma = \sqrt{1 + |\mathbf{u}|^2}$.

The Lorentz equations of motion:

$$
\frac{d\mathbf{u}}{dt} = \frac{q}{mc}\left(\mathbf{E} + \frac{\mathbf{u}}{\gamma} \times \mathbf{B}\right),
\qquad
\frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} = \frac{c\,\mathbf{u}}{\gamma}
$$

A multi-line derivation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega_\beta &= \frac{\omega_p}{\sqrt{2\gamma}}, \\
\lambda_\beta &= \frac{2\pi c}{\omega_\beta}.
\end{aligned}
$$
